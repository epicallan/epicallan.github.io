# Launch Plan

Early groups usually die from vagueness, not lack of interest. A fixed slot and a fixed format matter more than size.

## Step 1 — Fix the slot

- **Default:** Saturday evening, 5:30–6:30 pm (Allan's Sundays have the weekly deep-dive; weekday evenings clash with American work hours from 15:00).
- Same slot every week. Don't move it to suit one person.

## Step 2 — Fix the venue (free or cheap first)

In order of ease:
1. A friend's living room or your own place (for the first 4 weeks).
2. A quiet corner of a cafe at an off-peak hour.
3. A room at a wellness centre — ask Aguka Wellness Center or Ituze Center (see [05-venues-and-resources.md](05-venues-and-resources.md)); both already run mindfulness sessions and may host a small group.

Need: 3–6 chairs or cushions, quiet for 10 minutes, no music.

## Step 3 — Find a co-host

Allan spends 2–3 months a year in Kampala (stays of up to a month, at the start, middle and end of the year). A co-host keeps the slot alive while he's away. Ask one of the seed members after week 2 or 3. Give them the run sheet; that's all they need.

## Step 4 — Seed with 2–3 people you already know

Use the personal note in [02-invitation.md](02-invitation.md). Three people including you is a group.

## Step 5 — Open it up

Once the seed group has met once or twice:
- WhatsApp contacts and groups (where most East African communities actually form).
- Wellness centres' Instagram; LivinginKigali posts weekly events in its Friday Instagram stories.
- Kigali expat and young-professional groups.
- A short poster at Aguka / Ituze / Kigali Wellness Hub.
- Optional: a Meetup.com page (the Kigali listing has no group like this, so it would stand out).

## Step 6 — Run the 4-week trial

- Month 1 of the [reading plan](03-reading-plan.md) (Stoicism).
- Send each week's passage + prompt the day before.
- Log each session in [06-session-log.md](06-session-log.md).

## Step 7 — Review together (end of week 4)

Ask the group, in the open-question slot:
1. Do we keep going? Same slot?
2. What worked best — silence, passage, round?
3. Which school next? (Default: Epicurus.)
4. Who would co-host or take a role (timekeeper, reader)?

## While Allan is in Kampala

- Co-host runs the Kigali session with the same run sheet.
- Allan can attend the **Uganda Buddhist Centre, Bunga** Saturday session (3:00–5:30 pm) as his own weekly practice — see [05-venues-and-resources.md](05-venues-and-resources.md).
- Optional: join the Kigali session by video for the round (keep it audio-only and short so it doesn't change the room).
