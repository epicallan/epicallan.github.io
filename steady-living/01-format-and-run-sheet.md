# Format & Run Sheet

## The 60-minute session

| # | Block | Time | What happens |
|---|-------|------|--------------|
| 1 | Arrive and settle | 5 min | Phones away (in a basket, face down, or bags). Quick hellos. |
| 2 | Silent sitting | 10 min | Seated, eyes closed or soft gaze. One person keeps time with a quiet timer. |
| 3 | Passage read aloud | 5 min | One short passage from this month's school. Rotate who reads. Read it twice, slowly. |
| 4 | The round | 25 min | Each person ~4 min on the week's prompt. No advice, no cross-talk unless the speaker asks for it. |
| 5 | Open question | 10 min | One question from the passage, discussed together. |
| 6 | Silence and close | 5 min | One minute of silence, then one line each: *"What I'll carry into the week."* |

**Default prompt for the round** (when the reading plan's prompt doesn't fit):
> *Where did I hold the line this week, and where did I bend?*

If the group grows past 6, split the round into two smaller circles rather than shortening each person's turn.

## Ground rules (read aloud in the first few sessions)

1. **No creed, no recruiting.** Nobody has to believe anything. Religious people are welcome; nobody is converted to or from anything.
2. **A practice circle, not a debate club.** We test ideas against our weeks, not against each other.
3. **What's said here stays here.** Share the ideas freely; not other people's stories.
4. **No advice unless asked.** Listening is the gift.
5. **Pass is always allowed.** "I'll pass this week" is a complete answer.
6. **Start and end on time.** The hour is protected; it's part of the discipline.

## Facilitator script

You don't need to be an expert. The passage, the prompt and the round carry the session; you keep the time and the tone.

**Opening (after people settle):**
> Welcome. This is an hour to slow down and look honestly at our week, with some help from people who thought hard about how to live. We'll sit in silence for ten minutes, read one short passage, then go round. No advice, no cross-talk, and pass is always fine. Let's begin.

**Starting the silence:**
> Get comfortable. Eyes closed or resting on the floor. If your mind wanders — and it will — just notice it and come back to your breath. I'll ring the timer at ten minutes.

**Before the reading:**
> This month we're with [school]. Today's passage is from [author/text]. [Reader], would you read it twice, slowly?

**Opening the round:**
> Today's question: [prompt]. About four minutes each. I'll go first [or: whoever's ready].

Going first, briefly and honestly, sets the depth for everyone else.

**If someone gives advice or cross-talks:**
> Let's hold that — we'll leave space for each person in the round. Happy to pick it up in the open question.

**Closing:**
> One minute of silence. … Now one line each: what you'll carry into the week. … Thank you. Same time next week.

## Roles (rotate once the group is stable)

- **Timekeeper** — silence timer and round pacing.
- **Reader** — reads the passage.
- **Host** — opens, closes, holds the tone. Starts as Allan + co-host.
