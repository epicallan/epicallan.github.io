# Venues & Resources

From a web search on 2026-10-22. **Confirm times and details directly before relying on them** — listings in East Africa go stale quickly and many groups live on WhatsApp and Instagram, which search doesn't reach.

## Online

- [Modern Stoicism](https://modernstoicism.com/) — runs Stoic Week each year.
- [Meetup — Stoic Philosophy Practice Group](https://www.meetup.com/stoicism/) — can join online sessions.
- [The Best Stoicism Groups](https://whatisstoicism.com/stoicism-resources/the-best-stoicism-groups/) — directory of forums and Facebook groups (2020).

## Texts (free, public domain)

- Marcus Aurelius, *Meditations* — George Long translation
- Epictetus, *Enchiridion* — George Long or Elizabeth Carter translation
- Epicurus, *Letter to Menoeceus*, *Principal Doctrines*, *Vatican Sayings* — Bailey or Hicks
- *Dhammapada* — Buddharakkhita translation
- *Sallatha Sutta* (SN 36.6) — Thanissaro Bhikkhu translation (accesstoinsight.org)
- Aristotle, *Nicomachean Ethics* — W. D. Ross translation
