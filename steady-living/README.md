# Steady Living

A weekly hour of practical philosophy in Kigali. Silence, one short passage, and an honest round on how the week went. Stoic, Epicurean, Buddhist and other traditions, used as lenses on one question: **how do you stay steady when work, family and your own impulses all pull at you?**

No religion, no creed, no performance, no reading required.

## Why this exists

Kigali (and Kampala) have plenty of church and cell-group communities, but almost nothing for people who want a reflective, secular practice community. A web search in October 2026 found no philosophy or meditation circles on Meetup in Kigali (only tech groups), a few yoga and wellness studios, and one unverified Vipassana listing. The gap is real, so this group sets out to fill it.

## The core idea

- **Frame by question, not by school.** People join a practice, not a reading list.
- **The round is the heart.** The philosophy serves the honest check-in; it never becomes a lecture.
- **Small and fixed.** 3–6 people, one hour, same slot every week. Three people is enough to begin.
- **Four-week trial, then decide together.**
