Upper-Body Strength Plan — 3-Day

Gym Mon / Wed / Fri, letters rotate A → B → C · full session ~45–50 min, 1 hour hard cap · swim on the other days, one activity per day. Missed a gym day? Do that letter next, don't skip it. Tap a day to filter; drag the slider to change animation speed.

Progressive overload is the whole game. Each week beat last week — a little more weight or a rep or two on the main lifts. Log it in your WORKOUT.md; if you're not beating last week fairly often, you're not overloading.

Rest on a timer (agreed 2026-10-04): 2 min between sets · 3 min between exercises · supersets run as pairs: do both exercises back to back with no rest, then 2 min after each pair. That brings a day to ~45–50 min, inside the 1-hour cap. If you're running long, drop the last superset rather than rushing the compound. Protein 1.6–2.2 g/kg, 7+ hrs sleep: muscle is built during recovery.
