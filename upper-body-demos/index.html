Upper-Body Strength Plan — 3-Day

Gym Mon / Wed / Fri, letters rotate A → B → C · full session ~45–50 min, 1 hour hard cap · swim on the other days, one activity per day. Missed a gym day? Do that letter next, don't skip it. Tap a day to filter; drag the slider to change animation speed.

Program blocks

The plan runs in blocks of a few weeks, each reviewed at a Sunday deep-dive before the next one starts.

Block 2 · Mon 11-16 → Sun 12-13 (4 weeks)current
  • Fixed week: Mon C · Wed A · Fri B, plus Saturday volume and an optional Sunday light pull. Each weekday = main lift + 3 extras, inside the 1-hour cap.
  • Squat: heavy Monday 3×5 (starts at 40kg; +5kg when all 15 reps are clean), volume Saturday 3×8 at ~85% of Monday's weight.
  • Saturday bench: 50kg, earned by Day A hitting 4×5; aim 3×6, then 3×8. A volume day, not a second heavy day.
  • Arm boost: overhead triceps extension 2×10–12 with the lateral raise on Day A; hammer curl 2×10–12 with the rear-delt fly on Day B (left arm leads).
  • No lighter week up front. A lighter week comes at the end of the block, or sooner if two sessions in a row stall.
  • Missed weekday? Its exercises move to the weekend sessions; the weekday rotation carries on.

Progressive overload is the whole game. Each week beat last week — a little more weight or a rep or two on the main lifts. Log it in your WORKOUT.md; if you're not beating last week fairly often, you're not overloading.

Rest on a timer (agreed 2026-10-04): 2 min between sets · 3 min between exercises · supersets run as pairs: do both exercises back to back with no rest, then 2 min after each pair. That brings a day to ~45–50 min, inside the 1-hour cap. If you're running long, drop the last superset rather than rushing the compound. Protein 1.6–2.2 g/kg, 7+ hrs sleep: muscle is built during recovery.
