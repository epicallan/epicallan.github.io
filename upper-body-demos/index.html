Upper-Body Strength Plan

8-week, 5-day split · ~30 min sessions · supersets for volume. Tap a day to filter; drag the slider to change animation speed.

Progressive overload is the whole game. Each week beat last week — a little more weight or a rep or two on the main lifts. Log it in your WORKOUT.md; if you're not beating last week fairly often, you're not overloading.

Keep it to 30 min: ~3–4 min warm-up (light sets of the first lift), 60–75 s superset rest, ~2 min only on the heavy first lift, don't wander between sets. Protein 1.6–2.2 g/kg, 7+ hrs sleep — muscle is built in recovery.
