Fat-Loss & Full-Body Strength

8-week plan · 4 training days/week · full gym · goal: feel strong while the weight comes down.

About the 80 → 70 kg goal. 10 kg in 8 weeks (~1.25 kg/week) is aggressive. A sustainable, muscle-keeping rate is 0.5–1 kg/week — realistically ~4–8 kg in two months, with the rest following soon after if the habits hold. Chase the habits, not the scale number: strength training keeps the weight you lose as fat (not muscle), so you end up leaner and stronger, not just lighter. This is not about starving — if you feel faint, dizzy, or wiped out, eat more.

Nutrition drives the weight down

Training shapes and preserves; the deficit is what moves the scale. Aim for a moderate deficit — roughly 500–700 kcal/day below maintenance for about 0.5–0.7 kg/week.

  • Protein: ~120–140 g/day (a palm of protein at every meal) — this is what protects muscle in a deficit and keeps you full.
  • Plate: half vegetables, a palm of protein, a fist of smart carbs, a thumb of fats. Fill up on volume, not calories.
  • Liquids: water first; watch juice, soda, and sugary coffees — easy hidden calories.

The other daily levers

  • Steps: 8,000–10,000/day. Daily walking burns more over a week than any single workout.
  • Sleep: 7–9 hrs. Short sleep raises hunger and stalls fat loss.
  • Strength 2×/week (this plan) tells your body to keep muscle while you lose fat.
  • Weigh-ins: once a week, same morning conditions. Judge the trend over weeks, not day-to-day noise.

Weeks 1–2 dial in form and habits · 3–6 push the pace · 7–8 finish strong. Take a rest or gentle-walk day between sessions.

How to run the conditioning days: move through the exercises as a circuit with minimal rest, then rest 60–90 sec and repeat for the given rounds. Keep effort brisk but controlled — you should be working, not gasping to failure.

On the strength days: pick a weight that makes the last 1–2 reps genuinely hard with good form, and try to add a little each week. That progressive challenge is what keeps your metabolism and muscle up while dieting.
